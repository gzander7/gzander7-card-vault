    Download every card you own with its photo links, purchases, estimates, sold comps and value history.

    Estimates show the median and range of the latest two to five matching sales within 90 days. With at least four sales, prices above 2.5 times the median or below 40% of it are left out. Automatic sales come from CardPricer.

    High confidence takes at least four counted sales spanning two weeks, with the highest price under twice the lowest. Two sales, or a highest price at least twice the lowest, give low confidence. Other estimates have medium confidence.
